# PRODUCT.md

## Product Purpose
Roberto Ragno's personal academic portfolio site. Communicates credentials, publications, and research focus to peers, collaborators, conference organisers, and hiring committees in archaeology and related quantitative sciences.

## Register
brand — the design IS the product. This is a portfolio/landing page, not an application shell.

## Users
- **Primary**: Academic colleagues, potential collaborators, conference organisers in archaeology, environmental archaeology, and Roman/medieval history
- **Secondary**: Hiring committees, journal editors, grant assessors
- **Tertiary**: Members of the public interested in open-access archaeological data

## Brand & Tone
- Scholarly, understated, credible
- No marketing language; precision over persuasion
- Confident but not self-promotional

## Anti-references
- Flashy creative-agency portfolios with heavy animation
- Generic SaaS landing page templates (hero + features + CTA)
- "Academic portfolio generator" look — the al-folio default aesthetic with no customisation

## Strategic Principles
- Content is primary; design should not distract
- Accessibility matters: the audience includes screen-reader users and people in low-bandwidth environments
- The site must remain easy to maintain as a static Jekyll build
